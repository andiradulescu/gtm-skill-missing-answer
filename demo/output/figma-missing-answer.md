# BURIED ANSWER

## “Can clients comment on a prototype without creating a Figma account?”

**2 independent people asked this.**

### Source 1, evaluator

> “Wait, what? Clients have to create an account to leave comments? Non logged
> in users cannot provide feedback without registering? Is this still the case?”

<https://forum.figma.com/suggest-a-feature-11/guest-comments-voting-for-clients-w-o-account-19333>

The evaluator was trying Figma and said the account requirement would prevent
migration.

### Source 2, user

> “Do you know if clients can comment on a prototype without a Figma account?”

<https://www.reddit.com/r/FigmaDesign/comments/yqw5x5/how_do_i_share_a_prototype_with_a_client_that/>

The user had created a prototype share link and was checking the client review
workflow.

## What Figma’s agency page leaves unclear

The [Figma for Agencies](https://www.figma.com/agencies/) page promotes inviting
clients to collaborate and comment, but it does not state that clients must be
signed in to Figma and have view access before they can comment. The general
[UI Design Tool](https://www.figma.com/ui-design-tool/) page likewise promotes
comments without explaining this prerequisite.

## Verified answer

Clients must be signed in to Figma and have at least view access to the file to
comment on a prototype.

## Best place to fix

**Figma for Agencies → client feedback section.**

## Proposed copy

> Clients can comment on shared prototypes when they are signed in to Figma and
> have at least view access to the file.

This puts the prerequisite at the point where agencies evaluate the client
feedback workflow.

## Limitations

This is a dated snapshot, not a live finding. It does not establish what
onboarding screens a new commenter sees, which plan-specific invitation flows
apply, or whether Figma will add anonymous commenting later. No Figma workspace
or live page was modified.
